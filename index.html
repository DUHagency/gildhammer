What we won't

  • Anything that keeps someone safe. Brakes, steering, seat-belt parts, climbing gear, ladders, child seats, helmets. If someone could get hurt when it breaks, it is not printed here.
  • Food and drink contact. Layer lines trap bacteria, and most filaments are not food-safe. No drinking horns, no cups.
  • Things a store sells for less. Plain cups, hangers, basic phone cases. You would pay more for a worse part.
  • Somebody else's design for sale without leave. A model's licence decides whether we may print it to sell. Ours, public domain, or licensed for it.
  • Anything that could fire. No barrels, frames, receivers, slides, magazines, jigs or kits, and no prop with its orange marking left off unless a production holds a federal waiver for it.
  • Symbols of hate. Norse lore belongs to everyone who loves it. Symbols taken up by hate groups are not printed here, whatever the order.

The four questions

  1. Can you already buy it for under ten dollars? Then buy it.
  2. Is it custom, discontinued or niche? Good sign.
  3. Does it fit on a printer bed and print in a few hours? Then it is practical.
  4. Could someone get hurt if it breaks? Then we pass.
verk · the work

How an order goes

  1. 1

    Show us

    A photo of the broken part or the thing it has to fit, with a ruler in the shot, plus what it is for. Send it here.

  2. 2

    We measure

    We model it to your measurements and tell you the material, the price and the wait before anything is printed.

  3. 3

    We print

    PLA for indoors. PETG or ASA for heat, sun or the car. TPU where it has to flex, nylon where it has to take a hit.

  4. 4

    It fits

    Pick it up, or we ship it.
